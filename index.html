📅 14-Day Curriculum

The Full Training Roadmap

Seven phases, each building on the last — from blank canvas to a live revenue pipeline.

Phase 1 Foundations Days 1–2

Foundations & Stack Setup

Day 1

AI for Sales + Prompting Fundamentals

What LLMs do well and badly in a sales context. Map the full pipeline. Define your ICP and 2–3 buyer personas using AI — every search filter downstream depends on this.

LLMsPrompt EngineeringICP Design
Day 2

n8n Core + NocoDB Backbone

Canvas, nodes, triggers vs actions, credentials, expressions, and JSON. Set up NocoDB as the master lead database (Leads, Companies, Activities, Daily_Stats). Build your first webhook-to-CRM workflow.

n8nNocoDBWebhooks
Phase 2 Lead Agents Days 3–5

Lead Generation Agents

Day 3

Apollo — The Database Play

B2B search by title, industry, headcount, geography, and tech stack. Building targeted lists from your ICP, pulling verified emails, and exporting via API into n8n → NocoDB. Search-credit economics covered.

Apollon8n APINocoDB
Day 4

Apify — The Scraping Play

Actors as agents: Google Maps scraper, website/contact scrapers, LinkedIn-style actors. Running an actor from inside n8n, handling pagination, and structuring messy output into clean DB rows. ToS/legal segment included.

ApifyWeb Scrapingn8n
Day 5

Lusha + Waterfall Enrichment

Lusha for direct dials and verified emails. Then waterfall enrichment: Apollo → Lusha → Apify, take the first valid hit. Deduplication, phone formatting, and data-quality scoring to achieve 85%+ hit rates.

LushaWaterfall LogicData Quality
Phase 3 Signals & Analytics Days 6–7

Signals & Data Analytics

Day 6

News API + Trigger-Based Selling

Monitor companies for buying signals — funding, expansion, hiring, leadership changes, product launches. AI node summarizes each hit into a one-line "why reach out now" and writes it onto the lead.

News APIAI SummarizationSignal Scoring
Day 7

Lead Scoring Model in NocoDB

Fit score (ICP match) + Intent score (signals) = Priority (Hot/Warm/Cold). Segmentation, views, and data hygiene rules that keep the whole pipeline trustworthy.

NocoDBLead ScoringSegmentation
Phase 4 Dashboard Day 8

Sales Command Center

Day 8

The Live Sales Dashboard

Build the command center: lead volume by source, enrichment hit-rate, signal counts, outreach sent/delivered, reply rate, meetings booked, conversion, and avg response time. n8n writes daily stats automatically. Options: NocoDB views or a custom HTML dashboard embedded in Chatwoot.

NocoDB ViewsChatwootAnalyticsn8n
Phase 5 Outreach Days 9–11

Multichannel Outreach

Day 9

WhatsApp Outreach at Scale

Meta Cloud API, template categories and approval, bot/human handoff via Chatwoot, and safe bulk sending. Heavy compliance focus — a banned number kills the channel. Bot guards and opt-out handling built in.

WhatsApp APIMeta TemplatesChatwoot
Day 10

Email Outreach + Deliverability

Sending via Resend/SMTP and — the part everyone skips — deliverability. SPF, DKIM, DMARC, domain warmup, spam-trigger avoidance, list hygiene. Without this, the whole pipeline lands in spam. A/B subject lines built in.

ResendSMTPSPF/DKIM/DMARCA/B Testing
Day 11

AI Personalization at Scale

Combine enriched data + news signals + AI to generate genuinely tailored first lines — at volume, without sounding templated. Multichannel orchestration: WhatsApp and email sequenced intelligently per lead priority.

AI PersonalizationMultichanneln8n Orchestration
Phase 6 Follow-up Days 12–13

Follow-up & Conversion

Day 12

Automated Follow-up Engine

Multi-step sequences with Wait nodes, branching on reply/no-reply/bounce, auto-updating lead status, and stopping the sequence the instant someone replies. Human handoff so reps take over cleanly with full context.

n8n Wait NodesSequence LogicCRM Updates
Day 13

AI Inbound + Meeting Booking Agent

When a reply lands, an AI agent qualifies it, answers common questions, and books the meeting — or routes hot leads to a human with full pipeline context. State-machine pattern for clean handoffs.

AI AgentCal.com / CalendlyChatwoot
Phase 7 Capstone Day 14

Capstone Build & Governance

Day 14

Full Pipeline Live Build + Handoff

Each team builds the end-to-end flow live: scrape → enrich → signal-score → dashboard → outreach → follow-up → booking. Then the grown-up stuff: error handling, monitoring, compliance across UAE/India/EU, and a maintenance playbook so you own it after training ends.
